# Mir4Local v1.0.0

The first release. Track MIR4 boss spawns with live countdowns, reminders, and a checklist for every character.

## ✨ Highlights

- **Live countdowns** for every boss, in your own time zone. Bosses about to spawn glow orange and bosses that just spawned glow green.
- **Home dashboard** with what is spawning soon, what is live, your daily, weekly and monthly progress, and the next bosses to spawn.
- **Reminders** 5 minutes before, at, and 5 minutes after a spawn: a toast in the top-right corner of your screen (over your game, without taking focus) and an optional chime.
- **Tray mode.** Close the window and keep reminders running from the system tray, or quit completely. The app asks the first time.
- **A list per character.** Separate favorites and progress for each character, with IGN, class, server, alliance and clan.
- **Fast filters.** Dropdowns for type, cycle, map and area, plus favorites only, hide done and search. Only the list scrolls.
- **Random and respawn bosses.** Bosses without fixed times are labelled "Random spawn" or "Respawns after kill".
- **12 languages**, the ones MIR4 supports, and a light and dark theme.
- **Desktop app and web app.** Use the installer for the most reliable reminders, or open the web app in any browser.
- **Always current.** Mission data updates online and the desktop app updates itself.

## 📌 Good to know

- Toasts do not appear over games in exclusive fullscreen. Use windowed or borderless fullscreen.
- The app must be running for reminders to work. It can stay minimized or live in the tray.
- Random and respawn bosses show a label only. They have no countdown or reminders yet.
- On Windows, SmartScreen may warn the first time because the app is not code-signed: choose **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Coming from an earlier test build?** Uninstall it first, then install this one. Your characters, favorites and progress are kept.
